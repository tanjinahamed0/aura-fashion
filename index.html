The idea

Less, but better.

AURA started from a simple frustration: the shirts we actually wore out were the ones with nothing on them. No logo across the chest, no slogan we would grow out of — just good cloth, cut honestly.

So we kept the range deliberately small. Heavyweight cotton tees in a handful of colours that work together, and jerseys rebuilt from archive silhouettes we still like after forty years. Every piece is designed to be worn hard, washed often, and replaced only when it finally gives out.

01

Cloth first

Weights are stated in gsm and fibres are named. If a tee is 260 gsm open-end cotton, that is what you get.

02

No graphics

Nothing is printed on top of the garment. Pattern comes from the weave, the panel and the stripe — not an ink logo.

03

A small range

Two categories, twelve pieces. Short enough that we can finish every one properly, and re-run it when we get it right.
