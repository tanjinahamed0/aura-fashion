The beginning

Started with a plain black tee

We kept buying the same shirt and throwing the same shirt away. Either the collar went loose after four washes, or the black faded to a grey that nothing else in the wardrobe matched. The good ones cost three times as much as the ones that fell apart, and there did not seem to be a reason for that beyond the label on the neck.

So we started with one garment and fixed the things that annoyed us: heavier yarn, a ribbed collar that keeps its shape, shoulder tape so the neck does not stretch, and a dye that fades evenly instead of going chalky. Then we stopped adding to it.

The range is still small on purpose. Two categories, twelve pieces. Anything that does not earn its place in that list does not get made.

Philosophy

What we hold to

01

Nothing on the chest

No logo, no slogan, no print. Interest has to come from the cut, the cloth and the way a panel meets a seam — because that is the part you live in.

02

State the numbers

Every piece lists its fabric, its weight in gsm and how it fits. A catalogue that hides those details is asking you to guess, and guessing is how disappointment gets manufactured.

03

Fewer, longer

A small range gets finished properly and can be re-run when it is right. We would rather refine one tee across three seasons than release twelve we never wear.
