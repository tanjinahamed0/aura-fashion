The beginning

Winter, without the noise

Most winter clothing solves one problem and creates another. A hoodie that is warm enough to wear outside is usually too thick to close under a coat; a coat that is cut properly is usually lined with something that makes you sweat the moment you step indoors. We kept buying pieces that only worked on their own.

So the Winter Collection started from the layer underneath. Every garment here is specified by weight and fibre, and each one is photographed and described with the next one in mind — a crew neck that sits flat under a turtleneck, a coat cut wide enough to close over a hood, a long-sleeve tee light enough to wear alone on a mild December day in Dhaka.

The range is small on purpose. Five categories, twelve pieces. Anything that does not earn its place in that list does not get made.

Philosophy

What we hold to

01

Nothing on the chest

No logo, no slogan, no print. Interest has to come from the cut, the cloth and the way a panel meets a seam — because that is the part you live in.

02

State the numbers

Every piece lists its fabric, its weight in gsm and how it fits. A catalogue that hides those details is asking you to guess, and guessing is how disappointment gets manufactured.

03

Fewer, longer

A small range gets finished properly and can be re-run when it is right. We would rather refine one hoodie across three winters than release thirty we never wear.
